## Conventions

- One row per maintenance-log entry. This index is a **derived navigation map, not a record of authority**: the entry files and the two frozen logs govern; on any discrepancy the entry text wins.
- **Recount convention (X10):** row counts are recounted live from the homes at every pass that adds an entry, never carried from this file or a prior line.

- **Qualified citation (PKC-003 precedent):** MAINT-03..17 exist in BOTH logs with different content; citations in the colliding range must qualify the home, e.g. "MAINT-14 (LOG-1028)".
- **Date column:** the first date stated in the entry's own text. Two asterisked exceptions (MAINT-62, MAINT-65 → 2026-11-05) are manual overrides where the first stated date was a cross-reference; derivation disclosed in the MAINT-69 entry. "—" = not stated in the entry.
- **Known gaps, recorded not repaired:** MAINT-46 has no log entry in either log (landed by the successor custodian line; referenced in MAINT-47 and SESSION_STATUS); LOG-1028 begins at MAINT-03 (no MAINT-01/02 of that sequence exist in the repo); MAINT-63 and MAINT-64 appear out of numeric order in LOG-1029 as deposited; MAINT-54 and MAINT-55 are ID-collision entries (each spent twice in commit history; disambiguated in `Admin/AAudits/CORRECTION_2026-11-04_MAINT54_MAINT55_ID_COLLISION.md`).
